About Luminiferous

Luminiferous is a place to explore science and pass knowledge on: popular science articles for curious readers, and interactive lessons you can play with.

Why the name?

In the nineteenth century, physicists believed that light travelled through an invisible medium they called the luminiferous aether, a phrase that means "light-carrying". In 1887 the Michelson-Morley experiment found no sign of it, and the idea was eventually dropped. The name stayed in the history of science as a reminder that ideas are tested, and sometimes discarded. This site borrows it for a simpler purpose: to carry knowledge from the laboratory and the field to the reader.

What you will find here

Popular Science has articles on the ocean, climate and the Earth, written for general readers. They are currently in Malayalam.

Education has interactive lessons, where you can drag, slide and play to see how physics and earth science work.
